PUT YOUR PRODUCT PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER  (src/assets/products/)

- Name each photo after the product, exactly as listed in PHOTO-LIST.csv
    nike-zoom-stride-5.jpg
- Extra angles: add -2, -3 ...
    nike-zoom-stride-5-2.jpg
    nike-zoom-stride-5-3.jpg
- Formats: .jpg  .jpeg  .png  .webp  .avif
- Restart `npm start` after adding new files.

Photos you add replace the generic stock photo for that product automatically.
Only use photos you own or have a licence for.
